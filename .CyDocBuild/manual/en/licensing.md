## Application license {#license}

CyGameSDK source is MIT-licensed. Keep the root LICENSE and the notices in app/LICENCES when redistributing this foundation or a derived SDK. CyUI, Dear ImGui, ImGuizmo, nlohmann/json and CyToolsCore have their own retained notices. Lexilla/Scintilla/SciTE syntax data and bundled stb require their corresponding notices. Windows SDK/Direct3D terms and system-font licensing are separate from the application's MIT license.

The local documentation reader is CyDoc, under MIT. Its included notice applies to the reader, not to ownership of a game's files. This release does not include a game codec, licensed game content or an unrestricted right to redistribute third-party assets. Use authorized fixtures and preserve game-specific licensing constraints.
